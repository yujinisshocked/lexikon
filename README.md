# Lexikon

An all-in-one life tracking mobile app with mini functions that makes life easier.

---

## Description  <!-- What is this project? -->

**Lexikon** is an all-in-one life tracking app that aims to enhance lifestyle by replacing analog tools with one application. It is designed to be privacy-focused, fully local, and open-source, ensuring that all your data remains secure and under your control.
### **Features**
- **Note Taking**: Create, edit, and organize notes with support for images and basic Markdown formatting.
- **Habit Tracking**: Track your habits and monitor your progress over time.
- **Scheduling**: Schedule your tasks and events with ease.
- **To-Do List**: Manage your tasks and stay organized with a simple to-do list.
- **Shopping List**: Keep track of your shopping items and never forget what you need.
- **Budget Calculator**: Calculate and manage your budget to stay on top of your finances.
- **Financial Tracker**: Track your financial transactions and monitor your spending.

### **Technologies**
- **Flutter**: A cross-platform framework for building beautiful, natively compiled applications for mobile, web, and desktop from a single codebase.
- **Dart**: The programming language used to develop Flutter applications.
- **Hive**: A lightweight and fast NoSQL database for Flutter, used for local data storage.
- **Riverpod**: A state management library for Flutter that simplifies the management of app state.
- **Image Picker**: A Flutter plugin for selecting images from the device's gallery or camera.

### **Architecture**
- **MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)**: The app follows the MVVM architecture pattern to separate the business logic from the UI.
- **Repository Pattern**: Used to abstract the data layer and provide a clean API for data access.
- **State Management**: Riverpod is used for state management to ensure a reactive and maintainable codebase.

### **Privacy and Security**
- **Local Storage**: All data is stored locally on the device using Hive, ensuring that your data never leaves your device.
- **Encryption**: Sensitive data can be encrypted to provide an additional layer of security.
- **No Tracking**: The app does not collect or track any user data, ensuring complete privacy.

### **Open Source**
- **License**: The app is open-source and licensed under the MIT License, allowing for free use, modification, and distribution.
- **Contributions**: Contributions are welcome! Feel free to submit pull requests or open issues to help improve the app.
